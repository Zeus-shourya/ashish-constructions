About Shivani Properties

The half of the job
most builders leave to you.

A house is the easy part. What tires people out is the registry queue, the mutation that never gets entered, the seller whose title turns out to be disputed, and the bank that wants one more document nobody mentioned.

Shivani Properties exists so our clients never have to run that gauntlet alone. We buy, sell, rent and verify property in Rohtak, and we carry the file all the way through to a mutation entry in your name.

Being part of Ashish Constructions is the difference here. We are not brokers passing you along for a commission — we are the same people who will build on that land afterwards, and who will still be at the same booth in Rohtak in ten years if anything about it goes wrong.

Why Choose Us

What a tradition of trust
actually costs us.

Every one of these makes a deal slower or a commission smaller. We do them anyway — that is the whole point.

01

We will talk you out of a deal

If the title is murky or the price is wrong, we say so and lose the commission. A bad sale costs us more in Rohtak than it earns.

02

Backed by a builder, not a brokerage

We can tell you what a plot will actually cost to build on, because the people who would build it sit at the next desk.

03

Paperwork finished, not started

Registry is not the finish line — mutation is. We stay on the file until the record shows your name.

04

One commission, disclosed

You are told what we earn on the deal before you commit to it. No collecting quietly from both sides of a table.
