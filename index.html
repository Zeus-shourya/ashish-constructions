About Us

A builder who stays
answerable to your family.

Ashish Constructions is a family-run construction firm based at Forteasia Silicon Valley, Rohtak, working across Rohtak, Bahadurgarh and the wider Haryana belt. We take on residential and commercial projects from the first site visit through to handing over the keys.

Most of our work still comes from people who have already built with us once — which is why we keep the operation deliberately small enough that the person you speak to on the phone is the person accountable for your site. No layers, no shifting blame between contractor and agent.

Our property arm, Shivani Properties, carries the buying, selling and documentation side under the same roof — so a family can find a plot, build on it and get its papers cleared without ever leaving our office.

Why Choose Us

Six reasons people come back
and send their relatives.

01

Built once, built right

We would rather lose a job on price than win it and quietly thin the mix. What was quoted is what goes in — every bag, every bar.

02

The price on paper is the price

Your rate is fixed in writing before work starts. Any change is agreed and costed first — never discovered by you on the final bill.

03

Come and check the bags yourself

Cement, steel and fittings are named by brand and grade up front. Walk onto site any working day and inspect them — we would rather you did.

04

Build and paperwork, one office

Registry, mutation, loan files and NOCs run through Shivani Properties at the same desk. One team to chase, not four.

05

Rooted in Rohtak and Bahadurgarh

We live and work in these towns. Here a name travels faster than any advertisement, which is a stronger guarantee than a contract clause.

06

Investors

Put money into property
you can drive out and see.

Rohtak sits on the Delhi–Hisar corridor with a growing student and industrial population, and land here is still priced well below the NCR ring. We bring investors into plotted land and small commercial builds — the two things we know first-hand because we build them ourselves.

Title checked before it is offered

Every parcel goes through revenue-record and encumbrance checks before we bring it to you. If it does not clear, you never hear about it.

Build and exit under one roof

We construct it, Shivani Properties markets and resells it. You are not left holding an asset with no buyer pipeline.

Entry sizes that are not absurd

Plot-level participation starts far lower than a builder-floor ticket in NCR — sensible for a first property investment.

Photographs every month

If we are building on it, you get site photographs and a progress note monthly — whether you ask for them or not.

Talk to us about an investment

Tell us roughly what you are looking to put in and what you want out of it. We will come back with what is actually available right now — not a brochure.
